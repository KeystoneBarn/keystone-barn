Running a session

Left dial = left plug, right dial = right plug
  1. Check both dials are all the way counterclockwise, at the soft stop.
  2. Plug in the power cord.
  3. Plug in the coil. The left dial runs the left plug and the right dial runs the right. You can run both sides at once; they work separately.
  4. Turn on the green switch. When it lights up, the machine is running.
  5. Slowly turn the dial clockwise to bring the intensity up. You can turn it up or down during the treatment.
  6. When you're done, always turn the dial all the way counterclockwise to the soft stop.
