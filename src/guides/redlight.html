Hertz (hz)

How the light blinks

The only thing a mode changes. 10 Hz means the LEDs switch on and off ten times a second.

Depth comes from which device you strap where, never from the mode you pick.

660 nm · Red Light

Skin deep, on purpose

Visible red. Stops at 1–2 mm. Targets epidermis, dermis, fibroblasts and hair follicles.

Reaches
  • Wounds + proud flesh
  • Skin, coat + surface tissue
  • Scars and slow-closing cuts
850 nm · Near-Infrared

You won't see it working

Invisible. Reaches 3–5 cm into muscle, nerve and joint tissue.

Reaches
  • Joints, hocks + fetlocks
  • Muscle soreness + fatigue
  • Back pain and deep tension

Which mode, and when

Four delivery settings
🔆Default

Continuous

Steady, no blinking. Daily upkeep and everyday soreness. Start here.

〰️Tendon

Pulse 10 Hz

Blinks 10×/sec. Tendon and ligament work, per published repair protocols.

⚡Deep

Pulse 40 Hz

Blinks 40×/sec. Joints and muscle. Favored for deeper tissue work.

🌬Gentle

Breath

Ramps up and down slowly. Best for a nervous or first-time horse.
