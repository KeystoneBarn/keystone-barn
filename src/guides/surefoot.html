⭐ How do we know? The milestones
LEVEL UP! ⬆️
  • Calm & settled by the end — barely any swaying
  • Walks onto the pad on his own, no hesitation
  • Licks, chews, yawns, or drops his head
  • Wants to stay longer, session stretches out on its own
  • Same calm response, several sessions in a row
  • Leg feels lighter & easier to lift each time
STAY PUT ✋
  • Still swaying or bracing by session's end
  • Hesitates, backs up, or needs coaxing onto the pad
  • Anxious signs: head high, snorting, tense body
  • Wants off quickly, session staying short
  • Response changes a lot day to day — not settled yet
  • Leg still feels stiff, heavy, or reluctant to lift
